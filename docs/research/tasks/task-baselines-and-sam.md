Blocked by: none

## Question

Should detection and segmentation be separate initial workstreams?

## Resolution

Yes. Establish and evaluate detection and segmentation independently, then
investigate SAM and SAM3 to determine meaningful combinations. Do not assume
joint training or a specific fusion mechanism before reviewing this evidence.
Live Insula gates apply to every implementation milestone, beginning with M0.
