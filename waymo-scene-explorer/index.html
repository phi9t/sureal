Perception scene research preview

Two selected front-camera frames illustrate the environments in our multimodal perception research cohort. We are investigating detection and segmentation separately, with geometry and sensor alignment as foundational checks.

These are small illustrative extracts, not model predictions. Correspondence identities were checked against native projection records in live Insula. Supplied projections are used; rolling-shutter geometry was not independently recomputed. Non-TOP LiDAR geometry is uncompensated.
